The problem

At an education company with 149 Bitrix24 smart processes, hundreds of n8n workflows and a Metabase BI layer, automation knowledge lived in a few heads. Failures were found late, the same small requests kept coming back, and nobody had one place showing what ran, what broke and what to automate next.

The solution. A web app that reads the company's own systems, turns them into a living picture with a daily trail of what changed, what is breaking and what is worth automating, and puts an AI team to work on it.

The screens

What it does

01

13 AI teammates

Each has a role, a character and only the access it needs: n8n developer, Bitrix24 developer, Metabase analyst, researcher, QA, security reviewer and more.

02

Tickets, not chats

A lead bot plans the task, the right specialists do their steps, and the ticket stays open until the goal is reached. You can reply, redirect or stop it.

03

Approval first

Bots never change a system silently. Every change arrives as an approval card, is backed up and can be undone.

04

A live 3D office

The team works at their desks in a three.js office set in Amman, with day and night, meetings and a coffee break.

05

Bots that learn

From articles you give them, from release notes, and from their own finished tasks. Profiles show KPIs, levels and achievements earned from real work.

06

Bring your own model

Works with Claude, GPT and Gemini. The team picks a default, each bot can have its own, and every ticket shows its tokens and cost.

Also inside

  • Cost and speed by design: repeated jobs become quick recipes that skip planning.
  • Docs and boards: a Canva-style editor that draws any process straight from the system, with PNG and PDF export.
  • Fields and IDs: every field code, stage and list option ID, searchable and click-to-copy.
  • Safety: secrets stay on the server, every write is approval-based, logged and reversible, and people are deactivated, never deleted.
